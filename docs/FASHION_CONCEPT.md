# Fashion Concept

Public URL: https://pathai.name.ng/concepts/fashion-concept

A fictional retail prototype with separate Shop, Product and Bag views. Product hash links identify each item. The linen sample offers Stone/Natural photo variants and S/M/L/XL choices; the backpack has a Burgundy, One size option. The bag supports independent variants, quantity editing/removal, delivery or pickup, and a details/review/order preview. Choices stay in component memory between views. No payment, actual order, customer-data transmission or persistence occurs.

The studio homepage adds the concept to its existing horizontal business gallery. The enquiry link selects E-commerce and prefills a retail brief.

## Design and photography

Cobalt #294BD6, chalk #F4F3EE and ink #20211F. Self-hosted DM Sans, compact product views, native form validation and reduced-motion support. Images are compressed local WebP, served through Next Image.

Editorial sample imagery, not real inventory or a brand affiliation. Prices, sizes and delivery charges are illustrative.
