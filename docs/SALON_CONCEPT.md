# Salon Concept

Public URL: https://pathai.name.ng/concepts/salon-concept

A fictional beauty salon prototype with separate Home, Services and Book views. Services have category filters. The appointment preview has three steps: service, sample date/time and stylist, then review. Choices survive navigation between views; the browser Back button and hash links work.

Prices, stylists and times are illustrative. The preview makes no booking, payment or network request and does not persist guest details. The studio enquiry link selects Website and prefills a salon brief.

## Design and assets

Porcelain #FAF6F0, plum #68465E, lavender, muted blush and sage. Georgia headlines and self-hosted DM Sans body text, with short hover transitions and reduced-motion support. Service cards scroll horizontally on phones. The studio homepage adds this demo to its existing horizontal business row.
